About the Erasmus program

Erasmus+ is an innovative, EU-funded program that empowers students, educators, and youth to broaden their academic and cultural horizons through international mobility and cooperation. At its core, Erasmus+ facilitates opportunities for studying, training, and volunteering abroad by connecting your home institution with universities and organizations across Europe—and beyond. Participants can spend anywhere from two to twelve months overseas, engage in internships, take part in courses and job shadowing, or even pursue joint master's degrees. The program not only enhances academic development by allowing you to experience diverse educational approaches and cultural environments, but it also fosters personal growth, intercultural understanding, and the creation of long-lasting professional networks. To get started, students are typically nominated by their home institutions and provided with essential financial support to cover travel, living expenses, and other costs, making Erasmus+ a vital resource for experiencing transformative education and building a truly global perspective.
