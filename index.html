Welcome to the Erasmus Program

Welcome to our Erasmus Program application portal! We’re delighted you’re considering applying for this exciting opportunity. This page serves as your gateway to explore and submit your application for the Erasmus Program—a unique EU initiative offering students the chance to study, intern, or engage in academic mobility abroad. Here, you can find essential information on eligibility, the application process, and helpful resources. Our goal is to guide you step by step to ensure a smooth application process, from getting nominated by your home institution to submitting the final documents online. Enjoy your journey toward expanding your academic horizons and international experience!
