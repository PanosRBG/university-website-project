Requirements for the ERASMUS program.

For a student to participate in the program the following requirements must be met before proceeding to apply.

Requirements
1. The student must be a second year or higher.
2. The sum of all the passed courses relative to the student's total courses must be at least 70%.
3. The total score of the passed courses must be at least 6.5.
4. The student must have at least a B2 certification in English.
